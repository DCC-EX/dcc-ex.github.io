# New documentation coming soon!

Coming soon! A new, refreshed take on our documentation aimed at making
it easier for people to get up and running with DCC-EX.

While our website and included documentation has continued to evolve and
morph with our products over the years, the pace of development
continues to outpace the ability to maintain the documentation.

For those of you who have been using our products for years, you\'ve no
doubt noticed that there is a lot of detailed, highly technical
information available which is great for those who know what they\'re
doing, but it\'s very hard to comprehend for new people, especially
those who just want to plug in their new EX-CSB1 and enjoy running their
trains.

With this in mind, we\'ve decided to refresh our documentation from the
ground up. You will eventually still find it in the same place, but for
now you can preview it at
[https://dcc-ex.com/mkdocs-test](https://dcc-ex.com/mkdocs-test/), but
it will have a new look and the initial primary focus will be on
enabling those people who are new to DCC-EX and need to get started.

It\'s equally important we don\'t lose all the detailed knowledge gained
over the years, so the current documentation will be retained as the
[DCC-EX legacy documentation](https://dcc-ex.com/legacy-docs/). Over
time, we will incorporate this into the refreshed documentation also.

We\'d also like to say a huge thanks to those who have contributed so
much time and effort into the documentation over the years and continue
to do so, it is greatly appreciated!
