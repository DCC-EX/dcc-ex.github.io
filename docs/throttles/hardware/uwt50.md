Train Control Systems (TCS) makes a nice line of physical tactile
hardware based controllers. Since they support the \|WiThrottle
protocol\|, they work seemlessly with the \|EX-CS\|. Recently
preliminary testing on both the UWT-50 and the UWT-100 WiFi Throttles
are very encouraging.

Some of the major benefits of \|EX-CS\| for TCS UWT-Throttle users are;

- JMRI WiThrottle Server Protocol compliant.
- Direct WiFi connection of TCS UWT-Throttles to the \|EX-CS\|.
  (\|WiThrottle protocol\| is built into DCC-EX EX-Command Station. No
  JMRI connection is required.)
- Both DCC & DC Layouts can use TCS UWT-WiFi Throttles, and other
  smartphone WiFi Throttle Apps, together with \|DCC-EX\| and our
  TrackManager feature.

## Instructions

On the first screen, press the menu button:

![UTW-50 Network Options](/_static/images/throttles/uwt50_1.jpg)

From the main menu scroll down or press 6 for Network Options:

![UTW-50 Saved Networks](/_static/images/throttles/uwt50_2.jpg)

From the Network Options menu, select 2 for Saved Networks:

![UTW-50 select DCCEX](/_static/images/throttles/uwt50_3.jpg)

Scroll through the list and select the one that begins with DCCEX:

![UTW-50](/_static/images/throttles/uwt50_4.jpg)

The \|EX-CS\| has the ability to automatically upload EXRAIL Automation,
Routes & Command macros to the TCS UWT-Throttles.
